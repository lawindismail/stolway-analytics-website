Hazardous Areas

How To Read An Ex Marking

An Ex marking tells you where equipment may be installed. Here are the markings on our certificates, part by part.

Ex pxb IIC T4 Gb

ST Line Laser, Ex p
  1. ExExplosion protectedEquipment designed for use in explosive atmospheres.
  2. pxbPressurised enclosure, type pxProtective gas keeps the enclosure at overpressure; level "b" suits Zone 1.
  3. IICGas group IICThe most demanding gas group, including hydrogen and acetylene. Also covers IIA and IIB.
  4. T4Temperature class T4Maximum surface temperature 135 °C.
  5. GbEquipment protection level GbHigh protection: suitable for Zone 1 and Zone 2.

Ex db IIB+H2 T6 Gb

ST Line Laser, Ex d
  1. ExExplosion protectedEquipment designed for use in explosive atmospheres.
  2. dbFlameproof enclosureThe enclosure contains any internal explosion and prevents it spreading; level "b" suits Zone 1.
  3. IIB+H2Gas group IIB plus hydrogenSuitable for IIA and IIB gases, and additionally hydrogen.
  4. T6Temperature class T6Maximum surface temperature 85 °C.
  5. GbEquipment protection level GbHigh protection: suitable for Zone 1 and Zone 2.

Ex pzc IIC T4 Gc

Smart Sampling System
  1. ExExplosion protectedEquipment designed for use in explosive atmospheres.
  2. pzcPressurised enclosure, type pzPressurisation for Zone 2 locations; level "c".
  3. IICGas group IICThe most demanding gas group, including hydrogen and acetylene.
  4. T4Temperature class T4Maximum surface temperature 135 °C.
  5. GcEquipment protection level GcEnhanced protection: suitable for Zone 2.

Reference

Glossary Of Terms

CEMS
Continuous Emission Monitoring System. Measures pollutants in stack (flue) gas continuously.
OF-CEAS
Optical Feedback Cavity-Enhanced Absorption Spectroscopy. Laser absorption in a high-finesse cavity for ppb-level detection.
TDLAS
Tunable Diode Laser Absorption Spectroscopy. Scans a single absorption line of the target gas.
NDIR
Non-Dispersive Infrared. Infrared absorption measurement using optical filters rather than a spectrometer.
TFS
Tunable Filter Spectroscopy. Broadband light separated into wavelength components to resolve gas composition.
LOD
Limit of detection. The lowest concentration an analyser can reliably distinguish from zero.
F.S.
Full scale. The upper end of the measuring range; accuracy is often stated as a percentage of it.
T90
Response time to reach 90% of a step change in concentration.
ppm / ppb
Parts per million / parts per billion by volume (µmol/mol and nmol/mol).
Wobbe index
Indicator of the interchangeability of fuel gases, from heating value and specific gravity.
Isokinetic sampling
Extracting a sample at the same velocity as the gas stream, so particulate measurements are representative.
HMI
Human–machine interface. The local touchscreen used to monitor and configure the system.
