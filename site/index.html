Technology

Measurement Principles Behind Our Range

Each analyser is built on a proven measurement principle, matched to the application.

  1. 01

    OF-CEAS

    Optical Feedback Cavity-Enhanced Absorption Spectroscopy

    A laser locked to a high-finesse optical cavity multiplies the effective path length for ppb-level H₂O and H₂S detection.

    ST Line Laser
  2. 02

    TDLAS

    Tunable Diode Laser Absorption Spectroscopy

    Single-line laser scanning of the target gas absorption peak, free of background-gas interference, for up to five gases at once.

    ST LLE-9680
  3. 03

    TFS

    Tunable Filter Spectroscopy

    Broadband light sliced into wavelength components to resolve hydrocarbon composition in real time, with BTU and Wobbe index outputs.

    AP5-TFS
  4. 04

    NDIR

    Non-Dispersive Infrared

    Filter-wheel infrared absorption over a 14 m effective optical path for ppm-level carbon monoxide.

    ST CO-5300
  5. 05

    Laser scattering

    Laser forward scattering

    Isokinetic extraction into a fully heated chamber for dust measurement in wet, low-temperature flue gas.

    ST DMS-300
  6. 06

    Heated sampling

    Smart heated sample conditioning

    Heated probe, filter and 200 °C sample line with programmable blowback, keeping CEMS sample paths free of plugging.

    Smart Sampling System
