Privacy Policy

How Stolway Analytics handles personal information collected through this website.

Last updated 13 November 2026

Stolway Analytics, part of the Stolway group ("Stolway Analytics", "we", "us"), respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website, in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

01What we collect

  • Details you give us in an enquiry: your name, email address, phone number, company, project location and the information in your message.
  • Details you send us by email or phone.
  • Standard technical information our web host records when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

02How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, supply and support our products and services, and keep the website secure. We do not sell your personal information.

03Who we share it with

We may share your information with our related companies in the Stolway group in Australia, the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia when they are best placed to respond to you, and with service providers who help us run the website and handle enquiries. These providers may store information outside Australia. We take reasonable steps to make sure they protect it.

04Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we keep it only for as long as we need it.

05Access and correction

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@stolway.com.au.

06Questions and complaints

If you have a question or a complaint about how we have handled your personal information, email info@stolway.com.au and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Questions about your privacy?
