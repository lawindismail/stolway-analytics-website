Overview

Product Overview

System components

  • Smart Sampling Controller (SSC)
  • Smart Sampling Probe (SSP)

The SSS provides a solution to the persistent plugging problems that plague the CEMS application. The SSS is an innovative design to continuously deliver stack sample gas to any CEMS analysers and for use in severe service applications in Hazardous Area Locations. The integrated heating and blowback system extremely reduces the requirement for maintenance and provides reliable operation.

The Smart Sampling System (SSS) includes a specific PLC Controller with two HMI (12" at SSC & 7" at SSP) to monitor the entire SSS system, allow manual and remote changes to all essential system parameters & initiation of functions. The 7" HMI at the Smart Sample Probe (SSP) helps the operator diagnose the SSS system while at heights.

The SSS Controller (SSC) integrates accurate temperature management of its Smart Sample Probe (SSP) heated components, Sample Line as well as configurable sample system blowback functions to clean its Heated Sample Probe, Heated Sample Filter, and Heated Sample Line Tube using instrument air blowback operations.

At A Glance

Features & Applications

Key features

  • Can be integrated with any CEMS Analyser.
  • Modular design incorporating user programmable features for all sampling, auto blowback, and auto validation sequences.
  • Optional stack flow & temperature measurement for Mass Emission Monitoring.
  • Dual HMI at the SSS Probe (SSP) and SSS Controller (SSC).
  • User friendly HMI graphics, with live and history trends of all temperature zones, blowback pulses, validation pulses & CEMS Analyser readings.
  • Probe Tip Calibration for determining overall system health.
  • Reduce maintenance, boost sampling reliability, and increase operational uptime of the CEMS Analyser.
