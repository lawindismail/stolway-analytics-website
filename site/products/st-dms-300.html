Overview

Product Overview

Dust monitoring equipment developed for low-temperature and high-humidity working conditions, particularly suitable for monitoring ultra-low emissions in power plants.

The ST DMS-300 (positive pressure explosion-proof version) uses the laser forward-scattering principle. Particulates are extracted isokinetically from the flue into a fully heated gas chamber, where the forward-scattered light is measured and converted into an output signal proportional to concentration. It is designed for explosive gas or dust environments.

At A Glance

Features & Applications

Key features

  • Laser forward-scattering measurement of particulate concentration.
  • Isokinetic extraction of particulates from the flue (chimney).
  • Fully heated gas chamber for low-temperature, high-humidity flue gas.
  • Positive pressure explosion-proof version for explosive gas or dust environments.

Applications

  • Ultra-low emission dust monitoring
  • Wet / high-humidity flue gas
