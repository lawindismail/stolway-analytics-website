Overview

Product Overview

The ST LLE-9680 is a 19-inch panel-mounted tunable laser gas analyser designed for at-line industrial analysis and environmental monitoring.

Based on tunable diode laser absorption spectroscopy (TDLAS), the analyser scans absorption peaks of the measured gas, free of background gas absorption, and calculates concentration from the second harmonic and line-broadening information. It measures 1 to 5 gases simultaneously from O₂, CO, NH₃, H₂S, HCl, HF, HCN, CO₂, CH₄ and H₂O, with low detection limits.

At A Glance

Features & Applications

Key features

  • Universal structure, easy to integrate: standard 19-inch 4U chassis with modular internal components.
  • Strong anti-interference ability, high accuracy, small drift and high stability, using a low-noise semiconductor laser with single-line spectroscopy and wavelength scanning.
  • 1 to 4 laser transmitter modules supporting 1 to 5 customisable parameters on one screen, with short response time and high sensitivity.
  • Corrosion-resistant key components for petrochemical and other corrosive environments.
  • Sampling installation directly at the process pipeline, with no moving parts and high reliability.

Applications

  • At-line industrial gas analysis
  • Environmental monitoring
  • Corrosive process environments
